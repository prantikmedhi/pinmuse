How it works

One sentence.
Six moves.

“A poster for our Tokyo night guide.” Scroll to watch pinmuse make it.

  1. ExploreSearches Pinterest by phrase, pin or board, signed out and throttled.
  2. DistillBuilds a moodboard and reads its palette, light, warmth and motifs.
  3. PromptWrites the image prompt, with empty space where the type will sit.
  4. GenerateRenders with your model: gpt-image, Gemini, Flux, ComfyUI or none.
  5. ComposeSets real fonts with auto-fit, safe zones and grain.
  6. ShipHands back the file, a caption, hashtags and pin credits.

# in Claude Code, Codex or Gemini CLI
Make an Instagram story for our Friday
rooftop party. Get the vibe from
Pinterest: vaporwave sunset.

# pinmuse searches, builds a moodboard,
# generates, composes, and returns:
story-ig-story.jpg  caption.txt  manifest.json
# one shot: research → generate → compose
pinmuse create "rooftop party at sunset" \
  --inspo "vaporwave sunset aesthetic" \
  -t story -f ig-story \
  --title "Sunset Club" \
  --subtitle "Friday • rooftop • 8pm"

# or step by step
pinmuse moodboard "dark academia library"
pinmuse generate  "<prompt>" -f ig-post
pinmuse compose   -t quote --image out.png

FAQ

Questions.

Is reading Pinterest allowed?

pinmuse reads public pages signed out, cached and throttled to human volume, and never asks for your password. Use the official API for private boards, and check Pinterest's terms for commercial use.

Will it copy someone's pin?

No. Pins set the direction: palette, light and composition. The skill forbids reproducing pins, people, logos or watermarks, and every manifest credits the pins it learned from.

Do I need an API key?

No. Without one you get typographic designs on procedural backgrounds. A gpt-image, Gemini or Flux key gives photographic results like the gallery.

Which platforms and sizes?

Instagram posts, stories and reel covers, Pinterest pins, X, LinkedIn, YouTube thumbnails, Facebook covers, print posters, wallpapers, or any custom size.
